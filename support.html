Frequently asked questions

I bought “No Ads” but see ads after reinstalling

Open the store from the main menu and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase.

Will my progress transfer to a new phone?

Not yet. Level progress and settings are saved on your device only, so deleting the game or switching phones starts you from the first level. Purchases can always be restored.

How do I select units?

Swipe across your warriors to select them, then tap the ground to move or tap an enemy to attack. Double-tap selects all units or clears the selection. If you prefer drawing a selection box, switch Box / Swipe mode in Settings.

Can I play in portrait or landscape?

Both. Pick portrait, landscape or auto-rotation in Settings, and the interface adjusts.

A level is too hard

Mix unit types: knights in front, archers and mages behind. Scout with a small group first, because the fog of war hides orc towers. If you are still stuck, tell us which level. Your feedback directly shapes balance updates.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com for App Store purchases, or your Google Play order history for Android.

What data does the game collect?

Anonymous gameplay statistics and device information through Unity services, and nothing like your name or email. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
