Tiny Conquest

Privacy Policy

Tiny Conquest · Effective date: November 11, 2026

In short. Tiny Conquest has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. To improve the game and show ads, we use Unity services that collect anonymous gameplay statistics and device information. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Tiny Conquest (the “Game”) is developed by Lemons (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to alexeytarasenkov@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

The Game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any account credentials. There is no registration and no chat.

Information collected through third-party services

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Unity Analytics
Unity Technologies		 A random player ID generated by Unity; device model, OS version, language and approximate country derived from IP address; gameplay events such as sessions, levels started, won or lost, play time, tutorial progress, settings changes, ad events and in-app purchase events (product, price, currency). Understanding how the Game is played so we can fix difficulty, bugs and crashes.
Unity Ads
Unity Technologies		 Device identifiers (on iOS the identifier for vendor; on Android the advertising ID), device and network information, IP address, and how you interact with ads. Showing ads between levels, measuring them and preventing fraud. The Game does not request permission to track you across other companies’ apps on iOS.
Unity In-App Purchasing, Apple App Store, Google Play The product you bought and a purchase receipt. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google. Delivering “No Ads” and tip purchases and restoring them on a new device.

These services process data under their own policies: Unity Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, settings (screen orientation, selection mode, double-tap behavior) and the date of first launch are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to us and are removed when you delete the Game.

How we use information

  • To run the Game and deliver purchases.
  • To analyze aggregated gameplay statistics and improve levels, balance and stability.
  • To show ads to players who have not bought “No Ads”.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the data above to identify you personally.

Your choices

  • Ads. Buying “No Ads” in the Game turns off ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android) and limit ad personalization in Settings → Privacy & Security (iOS).
  • Deleting data. Email us and we will request deletion of the analytics data linked to your device from Unity. Deleting the Game removes all locally stored data.
  • Access and other rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data or object to its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

Analytics data is kept by Unity for as long as needed for the purposes above, according to Unity’s retention rules. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
