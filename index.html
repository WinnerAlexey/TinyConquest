About the game

A whole war in the palm of your hand

Classic RTS gameplay

Build. Hire. Command.

Raise a castle, collect gold, open barracks and a mage tower, and lead every soldier into battle yourself. No auto-battles: each march and each attack is your order.

  • Knights, archers and fireball-throwing mages
  • Command a single unit or the whole army
  • Fog of war hides orc camps until you scout them
Made for touch screens

An RTS you can play with one hand

The controls were designed for fingers from day one. Swipe across warriors to select them, tap to move, and use the minimap to jump around the battlefield. Hold the phone upright on the bus or sideways on the couch.

  • Swipe selection or a classic selection box
  • Tap the minimap to look, hold it to march
  • Portrait, landscape or auto-rotation
Key features

Why Tiny Conquest

Real base building

A castle, barracks, an archery range and a mage tower. Every building opens a new kind of fighter.

Swipe to select

Run a finger over your troops and they are ready for orders. Double-tap to grab everyone at once.

Minimap command

Tap to move the camera instantly, long-press to send your army across the map.

A campaign of 9 levels

Green meadows, scorching deserts and murky swamps, each with its own puzzle of defense and attack.

One-hand mode

The whole interface rebuilds for portrait, so you can command an army while holding a coffee.

Free to play

The whole campaign is free. A single “No Ads” purchase removes ads for good.
